frequently asked questions

what's in the box?

the base deck with 9 or 12 hot-swap sockets, keycaps, and a usb-c cable. modules snap on separately.

do i need drivers?

no. volos speaks hid out of the box and flashes over web serial — open the site, plug in, click flash.

can i use my own switches?

any kailh / cherry-mx compatible switch. linear, tactile, clicky — mix them however you like.

is it really open source?

yes. firmware, python library, and hardware files are mit on github. no account, no cloud, no lock-in.

how much does it cost?

the bom is $12–18. the base deck sells for $25–40. an elgato stream deck starts at $150.
